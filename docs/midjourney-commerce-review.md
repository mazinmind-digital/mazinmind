# Midjourney Commerce Review

This document records the legal and commercial review required before any artwork in this repository is offered for sale. It is a human-review checklist and must not be treated as legal advice or as evidence of rights clearance.

Important: all supporting evidence for ownership, payment plan, source-image permissions, and owner approvals must be stored privately outside this repository. Do not place account credentials, private prompts, receipts, personal information, or Midjourney job records in the public repo.

## Review policy summary

- Midjourney is a trademark of Midjourney, Inc.
- The visible product language must be truthful and not imply endorsement or sponsorship.
- Commercial sale requires explicit owner approval before `commercialUseApproved` can be set to `true`.
- Any artwork with missing evidence or unresolved IP concerns must remain blocked from sale.
- If there is any doubt, do not publish or sell the artwork.

## Required owner confirmation before launch

Before any sale launch, the owner must confirm in writing or in a private project record that:

- the originating Midjourney account and paid-plan history are known and confirmed;
- any revenue threshold analysis has been reviewed;
- ownership of every approved artwork is documented;
- permissions for uploaded reference images and depicted people are confirmed;
- trademark-safe product and SEO text is approved;
- the current Midjourney Terms and Trademark Policy has been reviewed;
- questionable or contested artwork is escalated for legal review.

## Mandatory public disclosure statement

Where a descriptive reference to Midjourney is displayed, use this visible notice exactly or with owner-approved legal counsel wording equivalent:

> MIDJOURNEY™ is a trademark of Midjourney, Inc. MazinMind is not endorsed by or affiliated with Midjourney, Inc.

## IP complaint workflow

If a complaint is received:

1. Pause the affected Shopify product immediately.
2. Remove purchase links from MazinMind pages and related navigation.
3. Preserve the complaint, provenance details, and review record in a private archive outside the repo.
4. Review the claim before re-publishing or selling the artwork again.

This workflow is not legal advice and is not a formal DMCA safe-harbor program without attorney review.
