## Social post 2: educational angle

**Headline:** An AI receptionist should know when to hand the conversation to a human.

A good intake workflow does more than collect a name and phone number.

It should:
- Ask only for the details needed to understand the request.
- Separate routine questions from urgent or sensitive situations.
- Avoid inventing prices or appointment availability.
- Make the human handoff obvious.
- Keep customer data and consent rules in view.

Our browser demo illustrates the intake concept. Real phone, SMS, calendar, and CRM integrations require separate configuration and testing.

See the demo: https://mazinmind-digital.github.io/Mazinmind-ai-receptionist-demo/

“This is a browser-based simulation of an AI-assisted receptionist intake flow. It is not connected to a live phone number, SMS provider, calendar, or production CRM.”

**10–25 seconds: Show the conversation**  
“Here, a customer starts with a common service question. The assistant gathers a few relevant details rather than asking for everything under the sun.”

**25–40 seconds: Show qualification and handoff**  
“The workflow can distinguish a routine request from something that needs a person. In production, those rules and escalation contacts would be approved by the business.”

**40–50 seconds: Explain integrations**  
“Phone, SMS, calendar, and CRM connections are separate implementation steps. We configure and test each one before using real customer data.”
