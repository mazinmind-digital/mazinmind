# Shopify setup and legal review

## Midjourney policy review section

The owner must confirm the following before launch of any art-print or apparel sale:

- The originating Midjourney account and the account's paid-plan history.
- Whether the business exceeds $1 million USD annual gross revenue.
- Ownership of every approved artwork.
- Permissions for any uploaded reference images and any depicted people.
- Trademark-safe product, collection, SEO, and metadata text.
- A current review of Midjourney's Terms of Service and Trademark Policy.
- Legal review for any questionable artwork.

## Sale approval gate

Commercial sale is blocked unless the owner explicitly approves the artwork list. The manifest in the app defaults to `commercialUseApproved: false` and must remain false until an owner-supplied approval list is recorded privately.

## Product and disclosure rules

- Clearly identify the work as AI-assisted or AI-generated.
- State that the customer is buying a physical canvas or T-shirt.
- State that no digital source file, copyright assignment, exclusivity, or intellectual-property license is included with the purchase.
- Do not promise copyright protection, exclusive ownership, or immunity from similar AI generations.
- Do not claim Midjourney guarantees title or non-infringement.
- Do not market the design as "exclusive," "one of one," or otherwise exclusively owned by the buyer unless the sale model and edition policy are explicitly documented and enforced.
- Replace "LIMITED RELEASES" language unless an edition cap is enforced by Shopify inventory and written internal policy.

## IP complaint contact

For intellectual-property complaints, direct the complainant to a private contact channel outside the repository and follow the pause/remove/review workflow documented in the Midjourney commerce review document.
