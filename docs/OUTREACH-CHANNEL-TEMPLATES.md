# Outreach Channel Variants & Follow-up Cadence

**Prepared:** 2026-11-04 | **Status:** Draft templates; not approved for automated or bulk sending.

## Email variants

Two email variants are maintained in [the Go-to-Market & Sales Playbook](GO-TO-MARKET-AND-SALES-PLAYBOOK.md). Use only with accurate sender identity, truthful subject, required postal address, a functioning unsubscribe mechanism, and a maintained suppression list. Replace all placeholders before use.

## Phone variants

### Phone A: short research opener
“Hi, this is [Name] with MazinMind Digital. I’m researching how independent [industry] businesses handle missed calls and website inquiries when the team is busy. Is the owner or service manager the right person for one quick question about your current process?”

### Phone B: operational workflow opener
“Hi, I’m [Name] from MazinMind Digital. We help small businesses map lead intake and follow-up workflows. I’m trying to understand how [Business] handles callbacks when the front desk is tied up. Is that something the owner or office manager oversees?”

If asked to stop, apologize briefly, end the call, and record the suppression request. Do not repeatedly call after a decline. Check applicable federal and state calling rules, do-not-call requirements, and business policies before any campaign.

## LinkedIn / social variants

### Social A: research invitation
“Hi [Name], I’m comparing how Greater Boston [industry] businesses manage missed inquiries and callback ownership. I work on practical CRM and AI-assisted intake workflows. If this is an active priority, I’d be glad to compare notes. If not, no worries.”

### Social B: workflow discussion
“Hi [Name], I’m putting together a short workflow review for local [industry] operators: where inquiries arrive, who owns the callback, and which requests need a person. I’d be interested in your perspective if this is relevant to your role.”

Do not scrape personal profiles, mass-message, imply a prior relationship, or treat a connection request as marketing consent.

## Inbound follow-up hypothesis

This cadence applies only to an inbound inquiry where the person reasonably expects a response. It is a testable starting point, not a universal proven schedule.

| Timing | Purpose | Exit conditions |
|---|---|---|
| Immediately | Confirm receipt and explain next step | Stop if invalid/duplicate or consent/expectation is unclear |
| Day 2 | Share one relevant practical insight | Stop on reply, decline, booking, or opt-out |
| Day 5 | Offer a useful readiness checklist or diagnostic | Same |
| Day 9 | Explain a low-risk pilot with scope and success metric | Same |
| Day 14 | Close the loop respectfully with an easy stop path | Stop after this touch unless the person re-engages |
| Day 30 | Selective re-engagement only when relevant and permitted | Suppression always wins |

Do not apply this sequence to cold prospects automatically. A website form submission is not blanket consent for promotional SMS or unrelated campaigns. Transactional confirmation and promotional nurture are separate purposes. SMS requires channel-appropriate documented consent and working STOP/HELP handling where applicable. If consent is unclear, use no SMS.

## CRM logging requirements

Record source, source date, audience segment, message/template version, channel, actual send/call date, delivery outcome, reply, next action, opt-out/suppression state, and responsible owner. Keep campaign records deduplicated. Suppression must be checked before every send and synchronized across providers.

## Pre-send checklist

- [ ] Channel is appropriate and lawful for this recipient and purpose.
- [ ] Sender identity, subject, message, and claims are accurate.
- [ ] All placeholders are resolved and links are tested.
- [ ] Required postal address and unsubscribe are present for commercial email.
- [ ] Consent and suppression checks passed for email/SMS/calls as applicable.
- [ ] No cold SMS marketing.
- [ ] Test delivery and mobile rendering completed using an approved test recipient.
- [ ] Stop conditions, retry limits, and failure handling are configured.
- [ ] Campaign is small enough to monitor replies, complaints, bounces, and opt-outs.
