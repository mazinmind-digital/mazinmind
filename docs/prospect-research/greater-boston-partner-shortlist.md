## Partnership pitch outline

**Working title:** Fewer Missed Inquiries, Better Follow-Through

**Audience:** Local service-business owners and operators.

**Problem:** New calls and web inquiries can be missed or left without clear callback ownership when teams are busy serving customers.

**MazinMind contribution:** A practical workflow review covering inquiry sources, response ownership, CRM capture, safe automation opportunities, human escalation, and measurable pilot criteria.

**Partner contribution:** Introductions, a member education slot, a co-hosted workshop, or a referral when a business asks for help outside the partner's core service.

**Proposed first collaboration:** A 30-minute educational session or co-branded checklist. No attendee list export, contact sharing, or marketing follow-up without the required permission.

**Referral handling:** Record the source and partner attribution in CRM. Ask permission before sharing a prospect's details. The prospect chooses whether to speak with either party.

**Commercial terms:** No referral fee, exclusivity, revenue share, paid sponsorship, or contract is offered by this outline. Any compensation, data-sharing agreement, or binding commitment requires CEO approval and written terms.
